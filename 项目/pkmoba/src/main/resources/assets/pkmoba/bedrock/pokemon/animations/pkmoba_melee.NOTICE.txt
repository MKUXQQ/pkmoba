Modifications for PKMOBA: isolated the physical animation into the unique pkmoba_melee group; divided animation_length and every rotation/position/scale keyframe timestamp by 2. Bone values, interpolation data, and the original motion are unchanged. The modified source is the adjacent pkmoba_melee.animation.json, distributed in readable JSON form in the mod jar.

This resource is used only when explicitly playing:
q.bedrock_primary('pkmoba_melee','physical',q.curve('symmetrical_wide'))
It does not replace Cobblemon's physical animation, model, poser, or other skill animations.

Duration: 1.5 seconds (original 3 seconds, exactly 2x).
Suggested server hit delay: 13 ticks. Right arm, forearm and hand converge on the original 1.25-second strike keyframe, remapped to 0.625 seconds. This is a timing inference from the authored keyframes, not a claim of client gameplay validation.
